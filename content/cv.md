## Professional summary

Senior AI and automation engineer with 6+ years across software development,
quality engineering and data engineering, now building production agentic
systems: multi-agent fleets, MCP server integrations, LLM-provider
abstraction with cost and quality controls, audit logging and CI gates. Prior
experience includes end-to-end QA for an FDA-cleared Class II women's
pelvic-health platform (mobile app, physician web portal, Bluetooth-connected
medical device) and municipal case-management systems with role-based access
control and Power BI reporting. Fluent in Python, FastAPI, Docker,
PostgreSQL, SQL and browser automation; comfortable owning systems end to end,
from architecture through client training.
